📊 Information Technology (IT)

Information Technology focuses on software systems, networking, cloud computing, databases, cybersecurity, and modern digital technologies used by organizations worldwide.

📘 Overview

Information Technology is one of the most in-demand engineering branches. Students learn how to develop, manage, secure and maintain software systems, databases, networks and cloud infrastructure.
