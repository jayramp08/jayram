⚙️ Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering deals with machines, manufacturing, automobiles, robotics, thermal systems and industrial design. It is one of the oldest and broadest engineering branches.

📘 Overview

Mechanical Engineering focuses on the design, manufacturing, operation and maintenance of machines. Students learn about mechanics, thermodynamics, production systems, robotics and industrial technologies.
