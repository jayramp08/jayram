📡 Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering

ENTC combines electronics, communication systems, embedded systems, signal processing and modern technologies like IoT, 5G and VLSI.

📘 Overview

Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering focuses on designing, developing and maintaining communication systems and electronic devices. It plays a major role in mobile networks, satellites, automation, embedded systems and modern communication technology.
