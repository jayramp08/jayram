⚡ Electrical Engineering

Electrical Engineering focuses on electricity, power generation, transmission systems, renewable energy, electrical machines, automation and smart power technologies.

📘 Overview

Electrical Engineering is one of the core engineering branches. Students learn about electrical circuits, power systems, control systems, electrical machines and energy technologies. Electrical engineers play a vital role in industries, power plants and modern infrastructure.
