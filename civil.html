🏗️ Civil Engineering

Civil Engineering focuses on designing, constructing and maintaining infrastructure such as buildings, roads, bridges, dams, airports and smart cities.

📘 Overview

Civil Engineering is one of the oldest engineering branches. It plays a crucial role in infrastructure development and nation building. Civil engineers plan, design and supervise construction projects while ensuring safety, sustainability and efficiency.
